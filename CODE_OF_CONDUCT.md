# Spark Plug community code

Spark Plug is built in public for people who want to run and understand their
own local AI systems. Be respectful, patient, specific, and honest about what
you tested.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, impersonation, malicious code, credential
theft, license laundering, privacy invasion, and deliberately deceptive claims
are not accepted. Do not post credentials, private prompts, outputs, node
addresses, personal data, or destructive proof.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that put people,
systems, licenses, or the community at risk. Report security issues privately
to security@gameworlds.ai. Report conduct concerns to support@gameworlds.ai.
Good-faith reports will be reviewed without requiring financial support or
community status.
